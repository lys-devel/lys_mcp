## Contributing

lys_mcp follows the same policy as lys. Please read [documentation of lys](https://lys-devel.github.io/lys/contributing.html) for details on our code of conduct, and the process for submitting pull requests to us.

When the communication protocol between lys and lys_mcp is changed, increment `PROTOCOL` in both `lys/localPlugins/Remote.py` (lys) and `lys_mcp/client.py` (lys_mcp).
